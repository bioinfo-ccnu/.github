# Code of Conduct

We aim to make bioinfo-ccnu a welcoming space for research, learning, and collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors respectfully, regardless of their background or level of experience.
- Discuss methods and results using evidence; offer constructive feedback.
- Credit others' work and respect intellectual property and data access conditions.
- Respect privacy in code, issues, pull requests, and discussions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberate disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting concerns

Contact the maintainer privately through [Lei Wang's contact page](https://wangleiofficial.github.io/contact/). Do not post personal details in public reports. The maintainer will review concerns and may request edits, remove inappropriate content, or restrict participation as appropriate.

This policy applies to organization repositories and their issues, pull requests, and discussions.
